How ATS Systems Really Read Your Resume

We validate against all three methods to ensure universal compatibility

1

Plain Text Extraction

The most universal method. ATS converts your document to plain text and reads it sequentially, top to bottom. This works because all file formats can be converted to text.

  • Converts PDF/DOCX to raw text
  • Ignores formatting and graphics
  • Most resistant to layout variations
2

Pattern & Keyword Matching

ATS looks for specific patterns using rules-based logic. Section headers, dates, email addresses, and phone numbers must follow recognizable formats.

  • Regex patterns for contact info
  • Section header recognition
  • Date format detection
  • Degree and certification matching
3

NLP & AI Parsing

Modern ATS systems use Natural Language Processing and AI to understand context. This is the most sophisticated method but not all systems have it.

  • Named entity recognition (NER)
  • Contextual skill extraction
  • Job title identification
  • Semantic understanding
